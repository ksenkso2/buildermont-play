Goal

Extract raw resources, move them on conveyor belts into factories, sell products in a Gold Vault, research the tech tree and finally build an Earth Teleporter. Producing the first Earth Token wins; the factory keeps running afterwards.

Rules

  • A belt pointing into a building feeds it. Any belt beside a building that does not point into it carries the building's output.
  • Extractors only work on deposits and never run out. Factories need a recipe (choose it in the recipe strip when placing, or in the inspector).
  • The Research Lab takes the items the selected technology needs, and banks anything a later technology will need — progress is kept per technology. The Gold Vault sells everything delivered to it.
  • Belt Splitters hand items round-robin to every neighbour; Underground Belts pair automatically with an exit in range; Robotic Arms move one item at a time from behind to in front (optionally filtered).
  • Power plants boost nearby production buildings while they have fuel. Demolishing refunds everything spent.
